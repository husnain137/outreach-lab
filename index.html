Frequently Asked Questions

The things most people want to know before reaching out

How long before I see results from LinkedIn outreach? +
Most clients start seeing booked calls within 2 to 3 weeks of launch. The 60-day guarantee means 10 to 15 qualified calls or we keep working until you get them.
Do I need a LinkedIn Premium account? +
For the Starter plan you need Sales Navigator — around $99 per month. For the Full Service plan we include it, so no extra cost on your end.
What kind of businesses do you work with? +
International B2B businesses — agencies, SaaS companies, consultants, coaches, and founders who want a consistent pipeline without running ads.
How long does it take to build a website? +
A basic portfolio site takes 3 to 5 days. A full business website takes 5 to 7 days from brief to delivery — includes revisions, mobile optimization, and free hosting setup.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No long-term contracts. Just 7 days notice and you are done. The goal is to earn your business every month through results — not lock you in.
